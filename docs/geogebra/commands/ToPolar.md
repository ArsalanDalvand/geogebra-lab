# ToPolar Command

#### `ToPolar( <Vector> )`
  Transforms a vector into its polar coordinates.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> `ToPolar({1, sqrt(3)})` yields _(2; 60°)_ in the  [Algebra View](Algebra_View.md) and _(2; stem:[\frac{\pi}{3}])_ in the
>  [CAS View](CAS_View.md).

#### `ToPolar( <Complex Number> )`
  Transforms a complex number into its polar coordinates.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> `ToPolar(1 + sqrt(3) * ί)` yields _(2; 60°)_ in the  [Algebra View](Algebra_View.md) and _(2; stem:[\frac{\pi}{3}])_ in the
>  [CAS View](CAS_View.md).

> [!NOTE]
> * The complex ί is obtained by pressing [.kcode]#ALT# + [.kcode]#i#.
> * See also [ToComplex Command](ToComplex.md), [ToExponential Command](ToExponential.md)
> and [ToPoint Command](ToPoint.md).
