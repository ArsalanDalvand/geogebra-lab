# Algebra View

## [#Algebra_View_User_Interface]# Algebra View User Interface#

By default, the  _Algebra View_ is opened
next to the 
_[Graphics View](Graphics_View.md)_. In addition, either the _[Input Bar](Input_Bar.md)_ is displayed at the
bottom of the GeoGebra window (GeoGebra Desktop), or an _[Input Field](Input_Bar.md)_ is integrated directly in
the  _Algebra View_ (GeoGebra Web and
Tablet Apps). The [_Graphics View Toolbar_](Graphics_Tools.md) is displayed at the top of the GeoGebra
window, with the

_Undo / Redo_ buttons in the top right corner.

The  _Algebra View_ is part of the
 [_Algebra
Perspective_](Perspectives.md), although you may [change the layout](GeoGebra_5_0_Desktop_vs_Web_and_Tablet_App.md) of GeoGebra's
user interface according to your needs.

## [#Creating_Mathematical_Objects]#Creating Mathematical Objects#

### Direct Input

In the  _Algebra View_ you can directly
enter algebraic expressions using the integrated [_Input Field_](Input_Bar.md) (GeoGebra Web and Tablet Apps) or
the _[Input Bar](Input_Bar.md)_ at the bottom of the GeoGebra window (GeoGebra Desktop). After hitting the
[.kcode]#Enter# key your algebraic input appears in the  _Algebra View_ while its graphical representation is automatically displayed in the

_[Graphics View](Graphics_View.md)_.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The input `y = 2 x + 3` gives you the linear equation in the  _Algebra View_ and the corresponding line in the
>  _Graphics View_.

### Commands

GeoGebra also offers a wide range of _[Commands](Commands.md)_ that can be used to create objects in the
 _Algebra View_. Just start typing the
name of a _Command_ into the _Input Bar_ or _Input Field_ and GeoGebra will offer you a list of _Commands_ that match
your input.

### Tools

Although the  _Algebra View_ doesn’t have
it’s own _Toolbar_, you can create [_Dependent Objects_](Free_Dependent_and_Auxiliary_Objects.md). Select a tool
from the _Graphics View Toolbar_ and click on any appropriate object in the  _Algebra View_ in order to create a new _Dependent Object_.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Create two points _A_ and _B_, whose coordinates are displayed in the  _Algebra View_. Select the  _[Line Tool](Line.md)_ from the _Graphics View Toolbar_ and click on both
> points in the  _Algebra View_ in order to
> create a line through points _A_ and _B_.

### Modifying Mathematical Objects

You can modify the algebraic representation of mathematical objects directly in the
 _Algebra View_.

Activate the  [_Move_](Move.md) tool and
double-click a [_Free Object_](Free_Dependent_and_Auxiliary_Objects.md) in the
 _Algebra View_. In the appearing text box
you can directly modify its algebraic representation. After hitting the [.kcode]#Enter# key, both the algebraic
representation in the  _Algebra View_ and
the graphical representation of the object in the  _[Graphics View](Graphics_View.md)_ will automatically adapt to
your changes.

If you double-click on a [_Dependent Object_](Free_Dependent_and_Auxiliary_Objects.md) in the
 _Algebra View_, a dialog window appears
allowing you to [Redefine](Redefine_Dialog.md) the object.

## [#Display_of_Mathematical_Objects]#Display of Mathematical Objects#

By default, mathematical objects are organized by _Object Types_ in the  _Algebra View_. In GeoGebra Desktop, you may use the [_Style Bar_
option](Algebra_View.md)  _Sort by_ in order to re-sort the objects by _Dependency_, _Layer_ or _Construction Order_.

> [!NOTE]
> * Hint:* You can collapse or expand each group of objects
> individually (e.g. all points, all free objects, all objects on a specific layer) by clicking on the plus or minus
> symbol in front of the group’s name.

### [Algebra View Style Bar](Style_Bar.md)

The [_Algebra View Style Bar_](Style_Bar.md) provides buttons to

* show / hide  [_Auxiliary Objects_](Free_Dependent_and_Auxiliary_Objects.md)
*  sort the list of objects by different criteria
* display additional 
[_Views_](Views.md) in the GeoGebra window (GeoGebra Web and Tablet Apps)

### Hiding Mathematical Objects in the Algebra View

If you want to hide the algebraic representation of an object in the  _Algebra View_, you may specify the object as an
[_Auxiliary Object_](Free_Dependent_and_Auxiliary_Objects.md). Open the tab _Basic_ of the
 [Properties Dialog](Properties_Dialog.md) and
check _Auxiliary Object_.

By default, _Auxiliary Objects_ are not shown in the  _Algebra View_. You can change this setting by selecting _Auxiliary Objects_ from the
_[Context Menu](Context_Menu.md)_ (right-click or Mac OS [.kcode]#Ctrl#-click), or by selecting on the appropriate
icon in the [_Algebra View Style Bar_](Algebra_View.md).
