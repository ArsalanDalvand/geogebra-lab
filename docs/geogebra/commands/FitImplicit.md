# FitImplicit Command

#### `FitImplicit( <List of Points>, <Order> )`
  Attempts to find a best-fit implicit curve of order n ≥ 2 through the points. You need at least stem:[\frac{n(n+3)}2]
  points.

> [!NOTE]
> See also the [ImplicitCurve](ImplicitCurve.md), [FitExp](FitExp.md),
> [FitGrowth](FitGrowth.md), [FitLine](FitLine.md), [FitLineX](FitLineX.md),
> [FitLog](FitLog.md), [FitLogistic](FitLogistic.md), [FitPoly](FitPoly.md),
> [FitPow](FitPow.md) and [FitSin](FitSin.md) commands.
