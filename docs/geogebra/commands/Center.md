# Center Command

#### `Center( <Conic> )`
  Returns the center of a circle, ellipse, or hyperbola.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> `Center(x^2 + 4 y^2 + 2x - 8y + 1 = 0)` (,
> : `Centre(x^2 + 4 y^2 + 2x - 8y + 1 = 0)`) returns point _A = (-1, 1)_

> [!NOTE]
> See also 
> [Midpoint or Center](Midpoint_or_Center.md) (,
> : [Midpoint or Centre](Midpoint_or_Center.md)) tool .

#### `Center( <Quadric> )`
  Creates the center of a quadric (e.g. sphere, cone, etc.).

> [!EXAMPLE]
> `Center(x^2 + (y-1)^2 + (z-2)^2 = 1)` yields _(0, 1, 2)_
