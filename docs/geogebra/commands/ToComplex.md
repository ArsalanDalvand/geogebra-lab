# ToComplex Command

#### `ToComplex( <Vector> )`
  Transforms a vector or point to a complex number in algebraic form.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> `ToComplex((3, 2))` yields _3 + 2ί_.

## CAS Syntax

#### `ToComplex( <Vector> )`
  Transforms a vector or point to a complex number in algebraic form.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> `ToComplex((3, 2))` yields _3 + 2ί_.

> [!NOTE]
> * The complex ί is obtained by pressing [.kcode]#ALT# + [.kcode]#i#.
> * See also [ToExponential Command](ToExponential.md), [ToPoint Command](ToPoint.md) and
> [ToPolar Command](ToPolar.md).
